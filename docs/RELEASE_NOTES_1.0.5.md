# Sonic the Hedgehog v1.0.5

- Keeps complete sprite artwork available during loading, boss entry and
  act-clear transitions.
- Preserves released animal artwork and smooth motion after native object slots
  are reused.
- Maintains the outgoing widescreen scene when leaving a level through debug
  mode, and prevents incoming scenery from appearing before its fade begins.
- Bridges the Marble platform loading handoff and corrects the full repeating
  pattern and finite extent of its vertical lava curtains.
- Keeps Star Light stairs on their original reset lifetimes while displaying
  their resting poses in wider margins. Corrects foreground tracking when the
  widescreen camera reaches its limit.

Fresh installations use adaptive Fit to screen; saved settings are retained.
**32:9 remains experimental.** Supply your own verified REV00 ROM. Save states
are build-specific; preserve native saves and settings when updating.
