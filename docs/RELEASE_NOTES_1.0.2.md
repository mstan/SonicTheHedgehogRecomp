# Sonic the Hedgehog v1.0.2

Corrects Marble Zone's invisible lava-hazard culling in widescreen. Hazards
loaded outside the original camera window now remain active until they leave
the expanded activation bounds. Lava damage uses the original collision and
hurt routines. Native 4:3 and recorded demos retain their original path.

Fresh installations use adaptive Fit to screen; saved settings are retained.
The **32:9 (experimental)** label remains. Supply your own verified REV00 ROM.
Save states are build-specific; preserve native saves and settings when updating.
