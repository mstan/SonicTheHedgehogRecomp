# v0.7.0 — Opt-in custom widescreen

Enable **Mods → Widescreen** in the recomp-ui launcher or the in-game Escape
menu. The mod is disabled by default and offers **16:9**, **21:9**, **32:9**
and **Adaptive**. Adaptive follows the entire window without a 32:9 ceiling.

- Separate custom renderer reconstructs foreground/background scenery beyond
  the Genesis tile buffers, with screen-anchored HUD and expanded rings/objects.
- Wider title-screen scenery and rotating special-stage layouts; full-width
  transition/fade canvas.
- Green Hill parallax follows the wide camera at stage boundaries, addressing
  the background slide near spawn.
- Object loading and culling share the same activation cells, fixing missing
  platforms and impassable pits seen in the initial widescreen prototype.
- Corrected the sound-driver return-stack unwind that restarted a stopped
  jump sound. This correction applies with either renderer.
- Saved mod settings; command-line widescreen overrides remain available.

Widescreen remains experimental. Extreme widths can exhaust dynamic actor
storage, earlier activation changes enemy encounters, and later zones/bosses
and save-state rewinds need additional coverage. The mod is disabled in netplay.
An existing Labyrinth Burrobot control-flow issue also reproduces in the older
native build and is not fixed by this release.

Windows x64 native build. Bring your own legally obtained Sonic 1 ROM: **no ROM,
save data, debug server, or development traces are included**. Source and the
required engine/core revisions are pinned in this release's submodules.
