# Sonic the Hedgehog 0.8.0

Adds optional 120 Hz, 144 Hz and monitor-refresh presentation. Choose High
refresh rate in Mods; Off remains the default. Movement and camera positions
interpolate while gameplay, audio and native animation keep their original
rates. Native output and adaptive widescreen are supported; finite menus and
special stages retain completed images.

The Audio menu also offers optional Unfiltered audio, disabled by default.
The separate enhanced FM path retains its own sound.

Carries forward the 0.7.5 shared-engine optimizations, ordinary native renderer
policy and default-on replayable feedback. The engine is pinned at
`0d27d77d3c4d72c58f0a6fd3278216e3b3edb4e2`.

The current-build Green Hill native/Fit Off-versus-144 comparison preserved
all 1,482 RAM frame logs, six raw image/RAM checkpoints and complete saved-state
bytes in each pair. Save/load and native rollback probes passed. The final
Windows package includes the existing feedback guide and reproduction helper.
This is representative coverage; no full-game or new platform certification
is claimed.

Windows x64. Supply your own supported ROM. Preserve native saves and settings
when upgrading; private feedback recordings, checkpoints and ROMs are excluded
from the public package.
