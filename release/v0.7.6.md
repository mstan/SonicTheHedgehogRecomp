# Sonic the Hedgehog 0.7.6

Fix the reported widescreen presentation problems in the title screen, level select, title cards, background scrolling, Final Zone, endings and credits fades. Recorded demos retain the original simulation while Marble platforms and glass pillars remain visible in the expanded margins.

Restore FM6 music correctly after a 1-UP, including in the special stage. Ending save states resume their current scene and remaining fade. The launcher shows Widescreen (experimental) explicitly and uses a window-scale dropdown; the selected sample rate persists.

Widescreen remains experimental and disabled by default. Five additional motion, ring and native-graphics reports were not reproduced in the bounded checks documented in the issue assessment; that is not a claim that those symptoms cannot occur on another route or setup.

Windows x64. Supply your own supported ROM. Preserve native saves and settings when upgrading. Old save-state compatibility is not required. Private feedback recordings and ROM data are excluded.
